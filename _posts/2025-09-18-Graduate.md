🎓 **A milestone reached, a journey celebrated.**

Today, as I stood during the 73rd graduation ceremony of Kharazmi University, it felt like more than just receiving a degree. It felt like the culmination of years of curiosity, late-night studying, and the profound realization of how much I love this science. I am officially a B.Sc. graduate in Geology!

Looking back, this journey has been anything but linear. It was paved with the grit of fieldwork under the sun, the meticulous precision of laboratory analysis, and the intellectual challenges of understanding the complex mechanisms of our planet. Geology has taught me that the Earth is never static—and neither should we be.

### The Pillars of My Journey 🏔️

No scientist is an island. My growth has been deeply influenced by the incredible community at Kharazmi. I have been fortunate to learn not just from textbooks, but from mentors who taught me how to *think* like a scientist.

I would like to express my deepest and most heartfelt gratitude to my professors, whose guidance has been my compass:
* **Dr. Ehsan Pegah**, for pushing the boundaries of my understanding.
* **Dr. Ali Misaghi**, for the invaluable insights and academic rigor.
* **Dr. Khalil Rezaei**, for the constant encouragement and support.

Your mentorship has not only shaped my knowledge of geological processes but has also fundamentally changed my perspective on science as a whole—as a pursuit of truth and a way of seeing the world.

### The Next Horizon 🌍

As I hang up my student cap, I am not slowing down. The transition from academia to the professional world is an adventure in itself. I am eager to take the lessons learned in the classrooms and the field and apply them to real-world challenges, whether through research, industry, or further academic exploration.

The Earth is vast, complex, and waiting to be understood. I am ready to start exploring it.

*Onward to the next chapter!*
