Sometimes, the best news arrives when you least expect it—often in the middle of a simple, joyful moment. 

It was during a wonderful lunch shared with Fariba that we received the incredible news: **Feztool.com** has been honored as the **Winner of the Merit Award (Ideator Category) at the 9th National Young Scientists Festival!** 🏅

<img width="2560" height="1920" alt="photo_2026-07-14_16-25-24" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d006a884-7e74-4f40-95ff-7cfda297db35" />

This recognition is more than just a trophy; it is a profound validation of the vision we have for feztool.com. It confirms that the problems we are tackling and the solutions we are building are truly resonating. This achievement reflects the tireless dedication of our team and our shared commitment to transforming ambitious ideas into impactful, real-world tools.

### Gratitude & Teamwork 🚀

A milestone this significant is never the work of one person. It is the result of shared passion and collaborative spirit. We want to extend our deepest thanks to the festival organizers and the jury for this honor.

Most importantly, we want to recognize the incredible people who make this journey possible. This success belongs to all of us:

*   **Mahdi Nedaee, Mohammad Kiani, and Parsa Elmi:** For your invaluable contributions, relentless hard work, and the seamless teamwork that drives this project forward.
*   **Professor Talkhablou:** For your mentorship, wisdom, and the guidance that has helped shape our path.

We are walking away from this festival not just with an award, but with even more fuel to continue our mission. Thank you to everyone who has supported us along the way.
